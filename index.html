OrbiGO Mobility prepares commercial EV leasing and battery swapping pilot in Nizamabad

The proposed service is designed for delivery riders and small transport operators. It is in pre-launch testing.

NIZAMABAD, TELANGANA — 19 November 2026 — OrbiGO Mobility Private Limited was incorporated on 12 August 2026. The company is developing a commercial electric vehicle leasing and battery swapping service in Nizamabad, Telangana.

The proposed rental model gives riders a way to access a commercial EV without buying one upfront. Vehicle availability, handover locations and final rental terms will be confirmed before customer bookings open.

What OrbiGO is testing

Vehicle access for local riders

The team plans to test whether flexible rental periods and battery replenishment suit local delivery and commercial transport work.

Rider onboarding and booking

OrbiGO is testing rider sign-in, booking and digital payment records. Production identity verification and fleet telemetry have yet to be verified.

Pilot status

OrbiGO is testing its software and prototypes before a proposed pilot in Nizamabad. The company has not opened the service to paying riders.
